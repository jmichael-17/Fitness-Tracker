Import weigh-ins

Copy the date and weight columns from Excel and paste them below, or upload a CSV. A date with no weight counts as a missed weigh-in and stays blank, and your 7-day average uses only the days you did weigh in. You see a preview before anything is saved.

Import training notes

Paste your notes as they are: an exercise line with the goal (like 4x5-8), then dated lines under it (like 10/12/26 165lbs - 8, 7, 6). Use | to split a line when the weight or exercise changes. Weights like 45+25 are added up and marked as plates. Dumbbell exercises are read as weight per dumbbell. Lines that need a look are highlighted, and nothing is saved until you press Import.

Backup and restore

Download a backup file with your weigh-ins, lifts, program and photos. Restore it here or in another copy of the app. Copy backup text skips photos so it stays small enough to paste.
